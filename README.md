# L3_T1_306_BusFaultCurrent_PSAF
This project focuses on the comprehensive analysis of bus fault currents and breaker 
ratings in power systems during Line to Line to Ground (LLG) and Line to Ground (LG) 
fault scenarios. Using MATLAB, the study involves the development of a detailed 
power system model and the implementation of fault simulations at various locations 
within the network.  
The primary objectives include the determination of fault currents at critical buses, and 
selection of appropriate circuit breaker ratings. The outcomes of the analysis provide 
crucial insights into the optimal selection of circuit breakers capable of interrupting fault 
currents safely, thus ensuring the reliability and stability of the power system. The 
project contributes to the broader field of power system engineering by offering a 
systematic approach to fault analysis and breaker rating determination. The findings not 
only enhance the understanding of fault behaviors in power systems but also provide 
practical recommendations for the implementation of protective devices, aligning with 
industry standards and regulations.
